## Purpose

Prevent dropped balls at shift change: missed broker promises, forgotten detention clocks, and “I thought you had that truck” gaps.

## Required written handoff fields

For every active load/truck:

- IDs (truck, driver, load, broker)
- Current status and last check-call time
- Next appointment and risk flags
- Open promises (who was told what, by when)
- Recommended next action

## Verbal overlay (short)

Use voice/chat only to emphasize judgment calls: sensitive broker politics, driver morale issues, or a re-power decision that needs nuance. Do not use verbal-only for facts that billing or the next shift will need later.

## Manager audit (weekly)

Spot-check three handoffs. If a new dispatcher cannot understand the board from writing alone, the SOP is not being followed — coach immediately.

## Takeaway

Handoffs are how a desk becomes bigger than one person. Write for the tired stranger who inherits your board at 05:30.
