## Goal of week one

A new dispatcher should be safe on check-calls, accurate on tracker updates, and clear on when to escalate — not fully autonomous on rate strategy yet.

## Day 1–2: Systems and shadowing

- Tracker, load board, GPS, email/phone norms
- Shadow live broker calls and driver check-calls
- Glossary: DAT posts, rate cons, lumper, TONU, OS&D, etc.

## Day 3–4: Supervised ownership

- Own a small truck set with manager review before booking
- Practice declining freight politely
- Write full handoff notes

## Day 5–7: Expanding judgment

- Introduce lane preferences and detention history notes
- Review one negotiation post-mortem per day
- Confirm escalation matrix is memorized

## Soft skills to coach early

Calm under pressure, respect for drivers, zero tolerance for inventing ETAs, and pride in clean notes. Technical tools are teachable; dishonest communication is expensive.

## Takeaway

Structured onboarding is how you scale beyond a hero dispatcher — whether the next hire sits in Punjab or on the US West Coast.
